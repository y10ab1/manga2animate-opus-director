# Brief for the Opus director agent (condition C)

You are the *director* of a manga-to-animation adaptation. You get manga pages and must deliver a
short animated video that is **faithful to the source manga** (characters, art style, plot, panel
composition) and **consistent across shots**. How you plan it is up to you: reading the manga,
writing a script, designing keyframes/storyboard, writing video prompts, reviewing your own outputs
(look at images and extracted frames), and retrying within budget.

## Inputs (in `<RUN>/input/`)
- `pages/page*.png` — the manga page(s), Japanese reading order (right-to-left, top-to-bottom).
- `panels/panel1.png … panel6.png` — the 6 target panels, **already in story order**. Shot *i* of your
  video must depict panel *i*.
- `characters/*.png` — one reference crop per main character (from the manga; black-and-white).

## Hard output requirements (needed for the automatic evaluation)
1. `<RUN>/final.mp4`, made with `tools/m2a_tools.py assemble`: **exactly 6 shots, hard cuts, in panel
   order**, each shot 2–4 s (total 12–20 s), 1280x720.
2. **No text on screen**: no speech bubbles, captions, SFX lettering, panel borders, labels.
3. Shots are generated by Gemini Omni in first-frame mode (the keyframe you give is frame 0).
4. `<RUN>/notes.md`: your script (per shot: what happens, dialogue, camera), key decisions, what you
   reviewed and changed, which candidate you picked and why.

## Tools (run from the repo root `/home/yuehpo/coding/manga2animate-exp`; every call is logged)
```
python3 tools/m2a_tools.py image  --run <RUN> --out <RUN>/kf/x.png --prompt-file p.txt --ref a.png --ref b.png [--aspect 16:9]
python3 tools/m2a_tools.py clip   --run <RUN> --out <RUN>/clips/x.mp4 --prompt-file p.txt --first kf.png [--last kf2.png]
python3 tools/m2a_tools.py frames --video <RUN>/clips/x.mp4 --out <RUN>/frames/x --fps 4   # writes sheet.jpg to look at
python3 tools/m2a_tools.py assemble --out <RUN>/final.mp4 --shot <clip>:<start_s>:<dur_s> ... (6 times, in order)
python3 tools/m2a_tools.py budget --run <RUN>
```
- `image` = gemini-3.1-flash-image (references are passed as `[Image k]` before your prompt). Use 16:9 for keyframes.
- `clip` = gemini-omni-1.1-flash image-to-video, 720p 16:9, usually 4–8 s; say the length you want in the
  prompt and pick the best `start:dur` window when assembling. Omni also generates audio (fine, not scored).
- Budget per case: **12 image calls, 12 clip calls** (same as the reference pipeline: 6 storyboard images +
  2 candidates per clip). Failed calls are free. Text reasoning and looking at images/frames is free.

## Rules
- Use only the files in `<RUN>/input/` as source material. **Do not open anything under `data/`,
  `third_party/`, or other cases in `runs/`** (they contain other methods' outputs).
- Work only inside `<RUN>/`. Do not edit `tools/`.
- Colorize the manga in a clean anime style consistent with the source designs; keep each character's
  identity identical across shots; keep each shot's composition close to its panel.
